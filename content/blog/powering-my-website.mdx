My personal website is built with this tech stack.

Here's the list:

- [Next.js 16](https://nextjs.org/): App Router.
- [MDX](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/guides/mdx): blog posts are written in Markdown.
- [shadcn/ui](https://ui.shadcn.com/) with [Tailwind CSS v4](https://tailwindcss.com/): components and styling.
- [shadcn Typeset](https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/typeset): the typography for posts like this one.
- [React View Transitions](https://react.dev/reference/react/ViewTransition): the page-to-page slides and titles that glide into place.
- [Motion](https://motion.dev/): the rolling framework names on the homepage.
- [Geist](https://vercel.com/font): the font.
- [Resend](https://resend.com/): sends messages from the contact form to my inbox.
- [Vercel](https://vercel.com/docs): hosting.
